The Ignite Club

JThe ignite club is the prinmary club in the Sri Venkateswara University College of Engineering, Tirupati. It primarily aims to enhance the students with the much needed marketing skills, other skills other than academics like the entrepreneurial skills, Leadership and organizational skills, other skills by igniting their hidden talents which would shape the future of our country. The Ignite club not only helps the students to enrich these skills, but also make its members a part of the strong network which would help them to grow in future
